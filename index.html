Source Resolution Status
NASA POWER ⓘ
NASA POWER
Daily reanalysis climate data: temperature, rainfall, solar radiation, humidity, wind speed — averaged over ~50 km grid cells from satellite observations and atmospheric models. ~50 km · Daily audited
MODIS NDVI ⓘ
NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
Satellite-measured greenness index (−1 to +1). Values >0.4 indicate healthy vegetation; <0.1 indicates bare or stressed soil. Computed from MOD13Q1 250m tiles updated every 16 days. 250 m · 16-day audited
SoilGrids 2.0 ⓘ
ISRIC SoilGrids
Global soil property maps at 250 m from ISRIC. Provides organic carbon (g/kg), pH, clay/silt/sand fractions, and bulk density for 0–15 cm depth — used for salinity risk and nitrogen estimates. 250 m · Static static
ET₀ FAO-56 ⓘ
Reference Evapotranspiration (ET₀)
Estimated water loss from a hypothetical reference grass crop. Computed from POWER climate drivers via the FAO-56 Penman–Monteith equation. Used to calculate irrigation requirements per crop. Derived · Daily audited
